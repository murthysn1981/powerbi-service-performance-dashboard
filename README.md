# 📊 Power BI Service Performance & TAT Analysis

## Project Overview

This project demonstrates the use of Microsoft Power BI to analyze service operations, monitor turnaround time (TAT), track pending calls, and evaluate service partner performance.

The dashboards provide operational visibility into service-call ageing, closure performance, regional trends, and pending actions to support data-driven decision-making.

## Business Objectives

- Monitor service TAT performance and identify overdue calls.
- Track daily pending calls and ageing categories.
- Analyze service partner productivity and closure performance.
- Monitor repeat calls, mobile closures, and parts usage.
- Identify operational bottlenecks and prioritize follow-up actions.

## Dashboard Modules

### 1. Regional Performance Analysis
- Regional service KPIs and call closure performance
- Calls received and closed
- TAT indicators and service trends
- Geographic distribution of service activity

### 2. Pending Calls & Ageing Analysis
- Pending calls by ageing bucket
- Call status analysis
- Service partner-wise pending workload
- Identification of cases requiring follow-up

### 3. Daily Service Performance
- Month-to-date performance
- Closure percentage and repeat-call indicators
- Field-actionable and customer-actionable calls
- Partner productivity and operational KPIs

## Technologies & Skills

- Microsoft Power BI
- Data visualization and dashboard design
- KPI development and performance reporting
- Service operations analytics
- Turnaround time and backlog analysis

## Business Value

The dashboards are designed to help service teams monitor performance, identify ageing cases, prioritize pending actions, and improve operational visibility.

## Data Privacy
